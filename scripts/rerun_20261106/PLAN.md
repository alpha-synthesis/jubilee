# Reruns of 2026-11-06: paired study under model 2.1.0 with the cash objective, and the parameter grid under the released generator

Fixed before execution (written 2026-11-06, launched the same night on the
48-core server). Code: branch `rerun/arxiv-2026-11`, the commit recorded in
`outputs/rerun_20261106/launch_state.txt`. Nothing in the archived outputs of
2026-11-03 (paired study, model 2.0.0) or of July 2026 (grid, generator
version 1) is modified; the reruns write only under `outputs/rerun_20261106/`.

## Why

The 2026-11-05 four-reviewer assessment of the manuscript converged on three
requests that prose cannot answer: (1) the benchmark score credits later
recoveries at the month-0 discount and the paper's own diagnostic shows that
simulated cash ranks the candidates differently, so OPE error, intervals and
selection under cash were untested; (2) the 190-run grid was generated by the
version-1 simulator (single stream, batch-pairing defect, thirty runs without
a source identifier) and never rerun under the released generator; (3) the
tabular annotation-augmented DR cannot react to annotations by algebra, so an
annotated direct method is needed to show what annotations can do.

## Protocols

| name | conditions | seeds | candidates | regimes | rewards | jobs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `paired` (`paired_cash.json`) | A_off, A_on, B_off, B_on (`configs/tmlr/`) | 7001–7020 | π_b, π_sms, π_visit, π_stage | tabular + gradient-boosted (frozen gbm block) | score, cash | 400 |
| `grid20` (`grid_v2_20seeds.json`) | grid cells 02, 04, 06, 08, 10, 16, 17 (`configs/grid/`, archived hazards) | 101–120 | π_b, π_sms, π_visit | tabular | score, cash | 700 |
| `grid5` (`grid_v2_5seeds.json`) | grid cells 01, 03, 05, 07, 09, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 | 101–105 | π_b, π_sms, π_visit | tabular | score, cash | 250 |

Shared settings, identical to the frozen 2026-11-03 protocol: one million
borrowers, batch size 50,000, 12 logged months, nine-month window, annotator
replicate 1 with bias and noise fractions 0.25, reference replicates 2–4
(three independent million-borrower populations per run), 200 bootstrap
resamples, selection resolution z = 1.96. The paired protocol differs from
the frozen one only in `name`, `out_dir` and `rewards` (recorded under
`rerun_of`). The grid protocols reuse each cell's archived hazard without
recalibration, so the realized recovery rate is a reported outcome, not a
gate.

Definitions. Score: `1{first recovery in months 0..8} × B0 × (1 − d0)`
(unchanged). Cash: the sum of `recovered_amount` over months 0..8, which
under full settlement is the single payment `B_t (1 − d_t)` at the month of
recovery. Both come from the same simulation; the logs, annotations and
references carry both columns, and estimation runs once per reward
(`panel.json` for the score, `panel_cash.json` for cash) through the
unchanged estimator code.

## Planned analyses (the paper's tables, per reward)

Per protocol and reward, `tmlr_aggregate.py` produces reference values and
Monte Carlo SEs, mean error ± SE and RMSE per estimator and target, interval
containment and width, support and clipping diagnostics, selection counts
and regret, paired condition contrasts and replicate-consistency ratios.
`dm_plus_from_extracts.py` adds the annotated direct method under the
protocol's corrupted annotator and under an exact annotator.

Comparisons fixed in advance:

1. Paired study, score: all 80 runs regenerated under 2.1.0 are compared
   field by field with the archived 2.0.0 panels (the equivalence argument
   predicts identical estimator outputs and references).
2. Paired study, cash: the same tables as the paper's Tables 4, 7, 11, 13,
   15, 21–24 under cash, with the achieved reference SEs reported against
   the score's targets (2.0 US$ values, 1.0 US$ gaps), not gated.
3. Grid, score: the same tables as the paper's Tables 5, 6, 9, 10, 12, 17,
   18 and Figure 2, now with independent references and measured reference
   SEs, plus the realized recovery rate per cell and the pilot deviation at
   strength 1.4 re-examined.
4. Grid, cash: the confounding ladder and selection under cash.
5. DM⁺: mean error ± SE per condition and target under the protocol
   annotator and the exact annotator, against DM, IS and DM⁺-IS.

What counts as success is not an outcome: every run completes, every
aggregate is produced, and the manuscript is revised to whatever the numbers
show. Post hoc analyses beyond this list will be labelled as such.
